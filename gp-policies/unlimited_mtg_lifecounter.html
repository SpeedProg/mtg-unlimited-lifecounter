Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ultimate Life Counter app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by SpeedProg ("Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Camera Usage & Object Detection

This Application requires access to your device's camera to function correctly. This permission is used strictly for the following purpose:

Tabletop Analysis: The camera scans the table surface to detect playmats and card zones. This data is used to automatically generate a user interface (UI) layout that matches your physical game setup.
  • Local Processing: All image processing is performed 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit and OpenCV.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not transmit, save, or store any images or video feeds on external servers.
  • Ephemerality: Image data is processed in Random Access Memory (RAM) and is discarded immediately after the layout is determined.

2. Data Collection & Third-Party Services

The Application does not explicitly collect personal data (such as your name or email address). However, it uses third-party libraries to ensure functionality and stability. These services may collect data used to identify your device.

Google ML Kit & Google Play Services

We use Google ML Kit for on-device object detection. Google may collect the following diagnostic information:

  • Device information (e.g., model, OS version)
  • App performance metrics (e.g., crash logs, latency)
  • Installation IDs (to measure unique installs)

This data is used for analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvements. It is not used to track you across other apps or websites.
